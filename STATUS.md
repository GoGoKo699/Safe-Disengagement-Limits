## What remains decisive

**Operational justification.** Identify one actual independent-fallback interface. Explain which changing state must be transferred, why the allowed invalidation pattern is defensible, what resource is shared, and why one completed handover releases it. Include sensing, actuation, communication, receiver capacity, and essential-service obligations. A generic VM migration analogy is not enough.

**Theorem-level novelty audit.** Compare the all-request-time recurring guarantee with replication, maintenance, and resource-dependent scheduling results. The checkpoint source reviews are targeted records, not proof that an exact predecessor does not exist. Record an exact reduction when found; do not infer absence from title or abstract searches.

**Structural extension.** Examine heterogeneous modules without assuming that the homogeneous serial order remains optimal. Separate a reduction to a post-request scheduling oracle from an explicit solution of that scheduling problem. A formula defined using an unknown optimum is not a closed-form frontier.

**Assumption sensitivity.** Preserve the proportional-invalidation alternative, initialization accounting, independent receiver budget, and distinction between cooperative withdrawal and sudden failure. If a result depends on a discontinuous worst-case loss rate, say so.
