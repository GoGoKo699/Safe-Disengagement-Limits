obligation. A mean update rate does not by itself justify pathwise fluid erosion.

## Concrete question

For one finite version replaced at exogenous times separated by at least
`Delta`, determine exact all-request-time readiness when every replacement
invalidates all preparation. Separate elapsed update age from amount prepared.
Assume full-copy duration `L=M/s<Delta`; begin cold at a verified update.

1. Prove the exit-time function over arbitrary causal allocation policies,
   including updates arbitrarily close to completion, simultaneous-event
   conventions, and supremum versus attained worst-case duration.
2. Invert it to obtain an exact readiness region and a matching long-run
   optional-throughput policy, with paid work and arbitrary allowed update
   histories. Test whether cold startup needs additional assumptions.
3. Compare with established sporadic-task/cache-refresh scheduling before
   calling an elementary utilization bound novel. A distinct uncertainty set
   is a model boundary, not a refutation of the earlier fluid theorem.
4. Determine whether independently sustained in-flight state and explicit
   response allowance can give a positive-delay interpretation of source
   departure. Keep ownership fencing, receiver provision, and pending
   obligations explicit; do not claim an implementation from a pipe model.
5. Add exact small arithmetic checks, record the result, and choose the next
   decisive question. Do not package an accumulation of variants as a paper.
