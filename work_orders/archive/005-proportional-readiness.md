# Pass 5: full-state readiness under proportional loss

22 September 2026. **Completed, with separate finite supporting checks.**

Question: can the smooth recurring problem be solved beyond one module, instead
of using the incorrect hot-support upkeep formula?

For homogeneous `M,a,gamma>0` and `s>gamma*M`, the aggregate state
`Z=M*completed+sum unfinished preparation` gives a sharp lower bound depending
on total initial preparation. Concentrating that total into hot modules and
one partial module attains it. Inverting the resulting time function gives an
explicit continuous upkeep frontier, with a matching all-history bound.

The result does not say every state of a given total is equally ready. It does
not solve the heterogeneous smooth ready-state problem. The next gate is the
specific prior-art and operational meaning of the surviving theorem, rather
than adding another parameter family.
