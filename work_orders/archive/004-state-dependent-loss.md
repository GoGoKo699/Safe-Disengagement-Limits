# Pass 4: state-dependent invalidation

22 September 2026. **Completed.** Prompted by the operational weakness of
full-rate loss at arbitrarily small preparation.

The integral potential proves ready/cold serial dominance for nonnegative
nondecreasing Lipschitz loss functions vanishing at zero. Strict capacity
thresholds and finite-time barriers are proved. A continuous non-Lipschitz
counterexample reaches the equality barrier, so regularity cannot be silently
weakened. The proportional stage formula is explicit.

The support-cost upkeep proof does not survive unchanged. The one-module
counterexample is retained. Pass 5 was therefore chosen to solve full-state
readiness in the homogeneous proportional family rather than relabel an
unknown ready-state optimization as an explicit frontier.
