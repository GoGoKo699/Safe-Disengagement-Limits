## What this pass establishes

- The [source comparison](2026-09-22-pass6-prior-art.md) excludes the
  fixed-capacity constant-loss and smooth-loss subclasses from the residual
  novelty claim. A scalar coordinate change gives a direct relation to old
  deteriorating-progress scheduling. Completion-dependent capacity remains the
  unmatched feature in the sources actually inspected. Full Glazebrook
  1992/1993 and the requested experience-based proofs remain access gaps.
- The [service interface](2026-09-22-pass6-interface.md) distinguishes
  acknowledged obligations, independent recoverability, receiver freshness,
  and response time. For the one-bit causal experiment, grace plus permitted
  response delay must cover information delay. Positive delay does not by
  itself force the source to remain alive when independent packets/logs suffice.
- The [residual-drain examples](2026-09-22-pass6-latency.md) give exact
  all-policy optima: overlap improves `10/3` to `3`; a separate three-module
  instance needs interrupted preparation to improve `38/9` to `37/9`.
  The drain is immutable old source work after ownership transfer. It does
  not solve the earlier propagation/fencing problem.

Internal independent-agent reviews checked the examples and caught two
interface accounting mistakes before integration: the minimum time to a nearby
independent log need not equal the minimum time to the responding receiver;
and an invalidation bound by initial preparation requires no new useful
preparation arrivals, including first copies. The note now states both
corrections. This is internal audit, not external peer review.

## Scientific decision

Retain the coupled all-allocation serial theorem as a narrowly stated candidate,
with its novelty unresolved. Do not defend the ideal fluid frontier as a
physical law or promote the delayed-release examples to a standalone paper.
They identify consequential assumptions and supply exact countermodels.

The next justified mathematical question comes directly from finite updates:
when changes are atomic and time-spaced, readiness must account for the time
since the last update. Pass 7 studies a one-version reset model with an explicit
event convention and cold initial state. Its purpose is to replace an
unjustified rate substitution by a proved finite-update benchmark. If its
recurring bound reduces to ordinary sporadic-task utilization, record that
reduction and seek the substantive question beyond it.

No manuscript, submission readiness, external endorsement, or release is
declared. The author need not approve this routine research continuation.
