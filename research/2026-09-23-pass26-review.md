# Pass 26 independent proof audit

23 September 2026. A separate agent independently derived the central
two-module argument and then read the complete
[common-drain proof](2026-09-23-pass26-common-drain.md). This is an internal
mathematical audit, not external certification or a literature priority
assessment. No mathematical correction was required.

**Finding:** the proof establishes attainment and every-minimizer
concentration under its two-module common-drain assumptions, including
design caps, nonnegative support charges, and the resulting nominal
positive-tolerance corollary. The result covers useful early capacity
release when `H>2*ell`. It does not establish serial optimality for every
fixed initial state.

## 1. Compactness and the robust quantifiers

The useful-input representation correctly removes reflection while retaining
all pre-handoff physical cap constraints. Extending an input by zero after
its own handoff makes the integrated auxiliary path well-defined on the
entire finite interval. Bounded inputs give weak-star subsequences and
uniformly convergent integrated paths. The input-zero constraints survive
converging handoff times, and the capacity steps converge almost everywhere
outside finitely many limiting release times. Testing against nonnegative
integrable functions preserves the limiting budget.

The terminal equalities also survive: uniform path convergence and
convergence of handoff times imply full preparation at each limiting
handoff. Cap constraints hold at all earlier times and at the endpoint.
Thus the limit supplies an actual schedule. Replaying its inputs and planned
handoffs under smaller losses is justified by reflected scalar comparison;
the same planned drain completions supply its capacity on every history.
This proves closedness of the actual-ready set without assuming attainment
of a time-optimal schedule infimum. Nonnegative support charges make the
design objective lower semicontinuous, so its minimum is attained.

## 2. The all-policy reduction at a putative interior minimum

Advancing handoffs to first full hits cannot harm any other module: it
deletes subsequent preparation of the handed-off module and weakly advances
its reservation release. The other recorded inputs remain feasible. This
canonicalization includes originally delayed handoffs and supplies the
strict pre-handoff cap margins used later.

When the first module has the smaller or equal coefficient, the proof uses
the first crossing of its discounted gap through a small positive level.
This choice is important: increasing its initial state by that level
produces exactly the earlier first full hit, without an unaccounted cap
crossing before it. Reassigning the removed interval of input to the second
module, with the matching discounted reduction of its initial state, keeps
that module below its original path until the old first handoff and equal
afterward. The resulting upkeep change is strictly negative, including for
equal coefficients and simultaneous original handoffs.

In the remaining fast-first case, any input to the later module before the
first handoff admits a small transfer to the first module, offset by
discounted initial-state changes. The relevant inequality is

$$\alpha e^{\alpha t}>\beta e^{\beta t}\qquad
(\alpha>\beta>0,\ t\geq0).$$

The compact support of the perturbation preserves physical cap margins;
strict design-cap room preserves initial feasibility. Unused capacity
similarly permits a reduction of the corresponding positive initial state.
Consequently the full-rate serial structure is necessary for this
hypothetical two-interior minimum. It has not been assumed for arbitrary
ready states or imported from the instantaneous-transfer theorem.

## 3. Deadline and cap checks in the final variation

The nondecreasing-input observation in Section 5 is valid. A subfull
unreflected scalar path cannot reach its cap at a finite time unless the
available input has become strictly larger than the full loss rate. If the
input subsequently never decreases, an earlier crossing would leave the
path strictly above the cap at the claimed later terminal equality. An
input jump does not jump the state. This excludes hidden intermediate cap
violations in the prescribed full-rate paths.

Extending the last handoff slightly therefore reduces its initial
preparation whenever that handoff precedes the common deadline D. After
establishing its equality with D, both initial states vary continuously with
the first handoff. Strict initial design-cap margins and the scalar path
observation give a genuine two-sided feasible neighborhood, even when the
first drain ends exactly at D.

On each smooth branch the remaining scalar objective has strictly negative
second derivative at every stationary point. At the release transition its
derivative jumps downward by `-beta*a*exp(beta*D)`. Neither possibility can
produce an interior minimum. This completes the contradiction; the opening
note's scalar calculation by itself would not have supplied it.

## 4. Precision and scope

In the positive-tolerance optimization, a nominal amount between zero and
epsilon is dominated by zero because its effective preparation is still
zero. Every remaining positive coordinate has nominal amount
`q_i+epsilon`. The conversion to design caps `(M_i-epsilon)_+` and support
charges `gamma_i*epsilon` is consequently exact. The theorem's local
perturbations stay in a fixed positive support, so those charges do not
interfere with the strict cost reductions. Concentration is a statement
about nominal preparation; effective coordinates need not be physically
full.

The degenerate cases `H<ell`, `H=ell`, and `s=0` are separately handled.
The proof settles two common-drain modules and leaves the unequal-drain
counterexample intact. It does not establish a general drain scheduler,
multi-module concentration, or robustness to unmodeled interface errors.

No numerical experiment or new finite checker is needed for this general
proof audit. Its substantive quantifiers are carried by the written
compactness, comparison, and perturbation arguments. Existing repository
verification remains a separate check on previously recorded fixtures.
