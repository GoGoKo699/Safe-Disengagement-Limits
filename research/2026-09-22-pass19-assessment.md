# Contribution assessment after precision and the general exchange

22 September 2026. This assessment follows passes 17–19 and preserves all
prior-art reductions and failed conjectures. It is an internal referee-style
assessment, not external peer review or a submission decision.

## Central candidate

A concise candidate claim is now: under unequal proportional deterioration,
minimum-maintenance preparation concentrates at its available bounds, even
when a positive request-time deficit prevents any module from being exactly
full at the request. The cost of readiness can nevertheless jump at a critical
exact-full endpoint. Thus the optimizer's concentration and the numerical
value of readiness have different robustness properties.

The original theorem covers every upkeep minimizer, arbitrary nonnegative
release parameters and all source budgets. Pass 19 extends the every-minimizer
statement to the one-sided envelope and to each feasible positive-tolerance
problem. The proof uses concavity of the intervening completion-time map;
it does not require intervening modules to be cold. The main paper retains
the simpler original proof and adds the stronger exchange where needed.

This is not an algorithmic result for the positive-tolerance frontier. The
old explicit frontier depends on full modules transferring at time zero and
on a cold suffix. Those features change under a deficit. Concentration alone
does not give the old recurrence or its real-evaluation complexity unchanged.

## Closest inspected work and reductions

The common-decay fixed-order problem, including its tolerance extension, is
ordinary linear allocation after exponentiating the common clock. The exact
Shioura–Shakhlevich–Strusevich correspondence and Wei–Wang–Ji restricted
recurrence remain prior-art reductions, not novelty evidence. Gehlot et al.'s
nonpreemption proof and older Glazebrook leads also prevent a broad claim that
seriality under deterioration is new. The bounded-storage upkeep argument is
established methodology.

Pass 17 inspected Choi–Park's complete fixed-deadline KKT proof. Its convex
inverse-power allocation block does not identify affinely with the exhibited
nonconvex S1 deadline region. A specified smooth common-clock reduction to
durable durations also fails. Those obstructions exclude particular routes;
they do not exclude coupled changes of completion-time variables or an older
theorem with the same exchange geometry. This is the next source question.

The new mathematical content being assessed is therefore the unequal-rate
exchange with maintenance prices tied to deterioration, and its robustness
under preparation caps. Do not claim arbitrary investment prices, a new
linear-allocation principle, a general controller-removal law, or a new
nonpreemption principle. No separate literature-priority search for the new
anchored-concavity argument is claimed completed by this assessment.

## Interpretation, negative findings and editorial decision

Independent receiver maintenance can remove the source-budget cost while
leaving total maintenance positive. Free early permanent transfer can remove
the standby problem. Zero source release retains the mathematical concentration
result. These exact counterpolicies identify which claimed service cost is
being optimized; they are substantive limitations, not footnotes to hide.

The critical precision fixture proves a nonvanishing upkeep jump from 1 to
7/6 with finite cold exit and a positive deadline. In contrast, the elementary
positive-margin inequality already destroys exact critical sustainability at
any positive tolerance. The paper distinguishes these facts. The precision
contract is a one-time request deficit, not a stream of normal disturbances
or an experimentally measured error model.

Keep the paper centered on the concentration theorem and its boundaries.
Do not add a general tolerance solver, more parameter families or a purported
physical application merely to increase volume. Continue a targeted primary-
source comparison of the stronger exchange, then reassess the contribution
and meaning gates. Submission readiness remains unestablished.
